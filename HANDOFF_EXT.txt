To allow: add to EXT_OK + NEEDS (downloads, declarativeNetRequest, webRequest) and make HeaderModes / DownloadsApi per extension (they are V77-only now: rules would apply to every request!). Do NOT just widen the regex.
- S8 todo: block nativeMessaging + MV2 in ExtRegistry.prepareSync; host permission filter for ext pages opened as tabs (TabManager still proxies every http(s) request of such a page); Diag rule for worker errors (console errors of a worker go to EngineHost log "ext <name>: ..." and Diag.w "engine" on crash only); Diag line for parked workers.
